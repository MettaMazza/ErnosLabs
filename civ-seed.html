Knowledge preservation · offline by design

Civ-Seed

Civ-Seed is a self-contained collection of practical knowledge prepared for situations in which its original website, institution or network is no longer available. It combines readable digital sections, a supporting library and condensed files designed for durable physical copies.

What the archive contains

Begin with the essentials, then preserve the route to rebuild.

The sections move from language, measurement and number into materials, energy, health, food, navigation, construction, communication, history and human experience. Each is written to remain useful when separated from the rest.

Understand

Language, measure and count

A starting vocabulary, body-based measurement, time, direction, angles and mathematical reference.

Sustain

Matter, fire, body and soil

Materials, heat, mechanical work, emergency care, agriculture, seed saving and food preservation.

Rebuild

Sky, construction and signal

Navigation, weather, shelter, sanitation, writing, printing, radio and maps.

Remember

History, warning and inner life

Records of social failure, patterns of capture and material intended to preserve reflection as well as technique.

Digital archive

Designed to be copied whole

The complete files can live on an ordinary computer or removable drive. Plain formats keep the content readable without a specialised service.

Physical record

Compressed for durable surfaces

Nine standalone text slabs retain the highest-priority material. A calculator helps plan how much surface and how many panels an engraving will require.

Open the stone series →

Why redundancy matters

A copy is useful only if another person can still understand it.

Preservation is not simply keeping bytes. Civ-Seed tries to retain language, ordering, instructions and practical context so that the material can be interpreted away from the place where it was written.
