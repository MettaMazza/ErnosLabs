About

Open science platform · public knowledge tree

A scientific model you can follow from its first question to its latest evidence.

Smithian Fold Theory is more than a manuscript. It is an open, versioned system of claims, dependencies, tests, controls and receipts. The public record is designed so that a reader can understand the argument, inspect the evidence and repeat the checks.

What has been built

A foundationally complete model, an open science platform and a connected map of knowledge.

Every scientific branch has reached foundational completion. The platform now lets that complete structure keep developing in public: start with a clear explanation, move through the connected claims, and open the exact research record whenever you want to inspect how a result was reached.

How verification works

A result is only as useful as the path that produced it.

The platform separates the question, the derivation, the comparison and the conclusion. That makes it possible to inspect where information entered and what a result actually establishes.

See this structure in the live claim record →
  1. 1

    Register the question

    Fix the claim, its dependencies, exclusions, candidate space, controls and intended evidence before reading the answer.

  2. 2

    Generate the alternatives

    Create the declared candidate space by rule and retain the complete census, including rejected alternatives.

  3. 3

    Run the controls

    False premises, changed sources, altered artefacts and broken boundaries must be detected rather than silently accepted.

  4. 4

    Compare with evidence

    Where a claim is empirical, preserve the registered source, measurement boundary, favourable rows and adverse rows.

  5. 5

    Keep the receipt

    Bind the accepted or rejected outcome to exact source and evidence identities so that later work cannot erase the history.
